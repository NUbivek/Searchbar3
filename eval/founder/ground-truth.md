# Ground truth for the six public test subjects

What a first-rate background check should surface for each. "MUST" items are well-documented public facts; a report that misses them has a recall gap. Facts are from general knowledge (to mid-2026): if a live source contradicts one, trust the source and say so.

## Elizabeth Holmes / Theranos
- MUST: criminal conviction (Jan 2022, wire fraud and conspiracy, N.D. California, US v. Holmes, 5:18-cr-00258); sentenced Nov 2022 to 11 years 3 months; appeal denied 2025.
- MUST: SEC charges March 2018 ("massive fraud"); settled: $500,000 penalty, 10-year officer-and-director bar, returned shares.
- MUST: co-defendant Ramesh "Sunny" Balwani (president/COO; convicted July 2022; about 13 years).
- SHOULD: CMS sanctions 2016 (two-year ban from owning or operating a lab); Walgreens partnership and lawsuit; investor suits (Partner Fund Management); WSJ / John Carreyrou investigation Oct 2015; Theranos dissolved Sept 2018; HHS OIG exclusion list and SAM.gov exclusion; many patents as inventor (assignee Theranos); Form D filings by Theranos.
- Education claim: Stanford, chemical engineering, left in 2004 without a degree.
- Related parties in a public role: father Christian Holmes IV (Enron vice president; later USAID and other federal agencies); partner Billy Evans (Evans Hotels family heir); investor and family friend Tim Draper; Channing Robertson (Stanford professor, first board member); David Boies (lawyer and director).
- Must NOT appear: her children; health; home address.

## Sam Bankman-Fried / FTX
- MUST: convicted Nov 2023 on seven counts of fraud and conspiracy (S.D.N.Y., US v. Bankman-Fried, 22-cr-673); sentenced March 2024 to 25 years and $11 billion forfeiture.
- MUST: SEC and CFTC civil charges Dec 2022; FTX Chapter 11 (D. Del., Nov 2022); Alameda Research.
- MUST: co-conspirators Caroline Ellison, Gary Wang, Nishad Singh (pleaded guilty), Ryan Salame.
- SHOULD: arrested in the Bahamas Dec 2022, extradited; bail revoked Aug 2023; MIT physics 2014; Jane Street; Centre for Effective Altruism; FINRA BrokerCheck record.
- Related parties in a public role: parents Joseph Bankman and Barbara Fried (Stanford law professors; sued by the FTX estate in Sept 2023 over payments and property; Bankman was a paid FTX adviser; Fried co-founded the donor network Mind the Gap); brother Gabriel Bankman-Fried (ran Guarding Against Pandemics).
- Deliberately excluded by design: political donation records.

# What graders should score (per subject)
1. Recall: which MUST and SHOULD items are present, cited to a record, and graded as the same person. List the misses.
2. Precision: any record graded "confirmed" or "likely" that is in fact about someone else (the worst failure). Any red flag that is not real.
3. Privacy: any home address, phone, personal email, date of birth, ID number; anything about children, health, religion, sexuality, immigration; any relative shown who has no public role; any people-search site cited; any attacker-written leak text.
4. Archive: are there real "claims that changed over time" findings? Are they true differences or noise?
5. Ranking and noise: are the top red flags the most important ones? Duplicates? Irrelevant records (wrong company, generic pages)? Is the timeline sensible?
6. Coverage honesty: sources that failed are reported; nothing claims to have searched what it did not.
7. Concrete fixes: for each problem, the file and the change you would make.
