WHAT THE SITE IS
Research Hub (diligence.bivek.ai) is a due-diligence tool for venture
investors. It researches a company, founder or market from cited sources,
and shows who in the visitor's own network can help them raise capital,
source deals or diligence a company.

THREE PROMISES
- Every statement is cited to a numbered source, or quoted from it.
- Funding and valuation figures are checked across sources and marked
  corroborated, single-source or conflicting.
- Contacts stay private to the visitor's account. They are never sent to an
  AI model or any other service.

TOP BAR (thin bar at the top of every page)
- Left: the "Research Hub" logo. Clicking it opens Open Research.
- Right: "Workspaces" (folder icon), "Watchlist" (bell icon), a small moon
  button, and the account button (the visitor's name, or "Sign in").

MAIN SWITCH (under the top bar, on the three main pages)
Three sections. The active one is filled in:
  1. Open Research  - research a company, founder or market
  2. Network Map    - the visitor's own contacts on a globe
  3. Founder Check  - a background brief on one person

NIGHT MODE
The moon button in the top bar switches the whole site to a dark theme. At
night it shows a sun; click it to go back to day. The choice is remembered
on that browser. Everything works the same in both.

EVERY PAGE
  Open Research        the home page
  Network Map          /network
  Founder Check        /founder
  Workspaces           top right; one workspace per deal (sign-in needed)
  Memo                 inside a workspace; the assembled deal memo
  Watchlist            top right; companies watched and their alerts (sign-in needed)
  History              from the account menu; saved searches (sign-in needed)
  Company profile      opened from "Company profile" buttons
  Sign in              Google only
  Privacy, Terms       links at the bottom right of every page

SIGNING IN
- Google only. Click the account button (top right), then "Continue with
  Google".
- Without signing in: Open Research, Founder Check, the Network Map and
  company profiles all work. Contacts imported while signed out stay in that
  browser tab only and are gone when it closes.
- Signing in adds: saved search history, workspaces, notes, data-room
  documents, memo download, watchlist alerts, and contacts saved to the
  account for every device.
- The site keeps only name, email and profile picture from Google and never
  posts on the visitor's behalf.
