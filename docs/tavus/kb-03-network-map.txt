TOPIC: Network Map: importing contacts, the globe, the dashboard, groups, and asking the network a question.
USE THIS WHEN the visitor asks about their contacts, the globe, groups, who can help them raise, source or diligence, or intro paths.

PURPOSE
See your own contacts on a globe and find who can help you raise capital,
source deals, or diligence a company or founder.

THE PAGE, TOP TO BOTTOM
1. The ask bar. Type a question about your network and click "Ask".
   Examples it offers:
     "Who can help me raise a fund?"
     "Who sends me deal flow in fintech?"
     "Who can diligence an AI company?"
     "Who do I know at Google?"
     "Intro paths to Stripe"
2. "Sources" card, four boxes:
   - LinkedIn: "Upload Connections.csv" (or "Replace export"). The small (i)
     explains how to get the file.
   - CSV / XLSX: "Upload contacts" for any spreadsheet of names and companies.
   - X: "Connect X"; once connected, "Import follows".
   - Attio (firm CRM): "Connect Attio - soon". Not available yet.
   - "Remove all" (red) deletes every imported contact.
3. One line of map controls: grouping ("By role", "By company", "By
   industry", "By country"), a "Find a person or company" box, and the count
   of people and companies.
4. The breadcrumb, for example "All > Founders & executives > Big Tech >
   Google". It shows where you are. Click a step to go back to it.
5. The globe on the left and the dashboard on the right.
6. Two bars under the globe: "Zoom to" and "Presets".
7. Answers to questions appear in a card under the map.

THE GLOBE
- A see-through globe. "You" are the bright point at its centre.
- Layers go outward: groups, then sub-groups, then companies, then people on
  the surface. Lines branch out from each to the next.
- Groups are spread all the way round. Ones on the far side show faintly;
  spin the globe to bring them forward.
- Each company is a burst of rays, one ray per person. The most senior
  people are closest to the company's centre.
- Hollow dots are firm contacts known through a colleague.

THE FIVE GROUPS (grouping "By role", the default)
- Investors & LPs: who can fund you or introduce an LP.
- Founders & executives: founders, CEOs, VPs, directors. They see deals
  first and can vouch for a founder.
- Operators & talent: people doing the work inside companies, plus students.
- Experts & advisors: lawyers, bankers, consultants, academics, clinicians,
  policy people, press. They can vet a sector, company or founder.
- Everyone else: contacts with no title and no company. Usually very few.
Inside each group, people are split by industry, then by company.

MOVING AROUND THE GLOBE
- Drag to spin. Let go while moving and it keeps turning.
- Scroll or pinch to zoom in and out.
- Click a group, company or person and the globe turns to it and flies in.
- Zoomed out, only the groups are named. Open a group to see its
  sub-groups; go closer to see companies; closer again to see each person.
- When you open a group, everything outside it fades.
- Four small buttons at the top left of the globe: zoom in, zoom out, fit
  to view, full screen.
- Hover anything to see its name.

THE DASHBOARD (right of the globe)
It always describes what is in view on the globe.
- "Layers": the steps, for example Role > Industry > Company > People.
  Click a step you already chose to go back.
- Seniority bar: Leaders, Heads, Team. Click a part to list only them.
- "Raise", "Source", "Diligence": how many people in view can help with
  each. Click one to list only them.
- "Next layer": what is inside, as bars. Click a bar to open it on the globe.
- People list: everyone in view, with title and company. It has a "Filter
  these people" box and sorts by Seniority, Recent or A-Z. The letters R, S
  and D on each row mean Raise, Source and Diligence.
- Click a person or company and their details open in the same panel, with
  "Company profile", "Search company", "Find intro paths" or "Founder check".

THE TWO BARS UNDER THE GLOBE
- "Zoom to": what is inside the part of the globe in view, largest first.
  Click one to fly to it. The arrow at the start goes back out one level.
- "Presets": "Whole network", "Raise capital", "Source deals", "Diligence"
  (each shows only the people who fit and brings that group to the front),
  and "Largest company".

GROUPINGS
- "By role": role, then industry, then company, then people.
- "By industry": industry, then role, then company.
- "By company": every company as its own burst.
- "By country": greyed out unless contacts have a location. LinkedIn's
  export has none; a spreadsheet with a Location column does.

FASTEST WAY TO ANSWER COMMON QUESTIONS
- "Who can help me raise?"   -> click "Raise capital" in Presets, or ask it
  in the ask bar.
- "Who do I know at <company>?"  -> type it in the ask bar, or type the
  company in "Find a person or company".
- "Who can introduce me to <company>?"  -> ask "Intro paths to <company>".
- "Who can vet <sector>?"  -> ask "Who can diligence a <sector> company?"
- "Show me my investors"  -> click "Investors & LPs" in the "Zoom to" bar.
