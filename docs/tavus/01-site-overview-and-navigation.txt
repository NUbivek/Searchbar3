RESEARCH HUB (diligence.bivek.ai) - SITE OVERVIEW AND NAVIGATION GUIDE
For the video guide avatar. Last updated November 9, 2026.

=====================================================================
1. WHAT THE SITE IS
=====================================================================

Research Hub is a due-diligence tool for venture investors. It helps a
managing partner or investor research a company, a founder or a market,
check every claim against its sources, and find out who in their own
network can help them raise capital, source deals or diligence a company.

Website addresses: https://diligence.bivek.ai (main) and
https://searchbar3.vercel.app (same site).

Three promises the site makes to users:
- Every statement is cited to a numbered source, or quoted directly from it.
- Funding and valuation figures are cross-checked across sources and marked
  as corroborated, single-source or conflicting.
- A user's contacts stay private to their account. Contacts are never sent
  to an AI model or to any other service.

=====================================================================
2. THE SCREEN LAYOUT (WHAT THE USER SEES ON EVERY PAGE)
=====================================================================

TOP BAR (thin bar at the very top of every page)
- Left: the "Research Hub" logo and name. Clicking it returns to Open Research.
- Right, in small grey text: "Workspaces" (folder icon), "Watchlist" (bell
  icon), and the account button (person icon with the user's name, or
  "Sign in" when signed out).

MAIN SWITCH (the dark pill-shaped switch under the top bar)
There are exactly three main sections. The active one is shown in black:
  1. Open Research   - research a company, founder or market
  2. Network Map     - see and search your own contacts
  3. Founder Check   - background brief on one person

FOOTER (bottom right): "Privacy" and "Terms" links.

=====================================================================
3. SITE MAP (EVERY PAGE)
=====================================================================

Main sections (in the switch):
  /                  Open Research

=====================================================================
4. SIGNING IN
=====================================================================

- Sign-in is with Google only. Click the account button (top right), then
  "Continue with Google".
- Without signing in, a user can still use Open Research, Founder Check, the
  Network Map and company profiles. Contacts imported while signed out stay
  only in that browser tab and disappear when it closes.
- Signing in adds: saved search history, deal workspaces, notes, data-room
  documents, memo export, watchlists with alerts, and contacts saved to the
  account so they are there on every device.
- The site stores only name, email and profile picture from Google, and
  never posts anything on the user's behalf.

=====================================================================
5. OPEN RESEARCH (the home page, "/")
=====================================================================

PURPOSE: Ask a question about any company, founder or market and get an
answer written only from cited sources.

WHAT IS ON THE PAGE, top to bottom:
1. Heading: "Research any company, founder or market".
2. The search bar (large box with a magnifier icon) and the black
   "Search" button. Example: type "Anthropic funding" and press Enter.
3. A quiet line of options under the search bar:
   - "Published" date filter: Any time, Past month, Past year, Past 3 years.
   - "Save to" (signed in only): file the search under a deal workspace, or
     keep it in History only.
   - "Add new workspace" link (opens Workspaces).
4. The source row (chips the user can switch on and off):
   - "All": selects every source, including Custom. Click again to clear.
   - "Curated sources": about 800 hand-picked VC, accelerator, funding-news,
     government and research feeds. On by default.
   - "Web": general web and news search. On by default.
   - "LinkedIn", "X", "Reddit", "Substack", "Medium", "Crunchbase",
     "Pitchbook", "Carta": searches restricted to those websites.
   - "Verified": official filings and market data (SEC EDGAR, financial
     market data, and government sites).
   - "Gov & regulators": SEC, Federal Reserve and FRED, FDIC, OCC, Treasury,
     CFPB, FTC, DOJ, FINRA, CMS, FDA, HHS, NIH, Census, BLS, BEA, SBA, state
     business registries (including Delaware, California, New York, Texas,
     Louisiana), and UK and EU regulators.
   - "Patents & IP": USPTO, Google Patents, US Copyright Office, WIPO, EPO,
     UK IPO; and research funders DARPA, ARPA-E, IARPA, NSF, NIST, NASA,
     NIH RePORTER, SBIR/STTR awards.
   - "Institutions": UN, World Bank, IMF, OECD, WHO, World Economic Forum;
     McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, Gartner, Forrester;
     Brookings, RAND, Pew, NBER, CFR; SSRN, arXiv, CB Insights, Statista, HBR.
   - "Custom": add your own web addresses (URLs) or upload files (PDF, DOCX,
     CSV, TXT; up to 5 files, 25 MB each) to be searched with the rest.
   Hovering over a source chip shows what it covers.

AFTER A SEARCH, the results show:
- An "AI" badge with the model's name when an AI wrote the answer, or
  "Quoted from sources" when the answer is made of direct quotes.
- A confidence chip (high, medium, low).
- The answer in five sections: Summary, Key Points, Detailed Analysis,
  Different Perspectives, Follow-up Questions. Every sentence ends with a
  citation such as [Source 3].
- The claim check: funding and valuation figures marked corroborated (two
  or more sites agree), single-source, or conflicting.
- The ranked source list, each with a quality badge (Filing / official,
  Reputable press, Blog / newsletter, Forum / social) and a date.
- "Your network and <company>": who the user knows at that company, at its
  investors and competitors, and which experts and investors in their
  network cover that sector.
- Buttons to open the Company profile or run a Founder check.

6. NETWORK MAP ("/network")
=====================================================================

PURPOSE: See your own contacts as a map and find who can help you raise
capital, source deals, or diligence a company or founder.

WHAT IS ON THE PAGE, top to bottom:
1. The "Ask your network" search bar. Its grey placeholder rotates through
   example questions; clicking into the empty box shows all five:
     "Who can help me raise a fund?"
     "Who sends me deal flow in fintech?"
     "Who can diligence an AI company?"
     "Who do I know at Google?"
     "Intro paths to Stripe"
2. "Sources" card: total contacts and four cards in one row:
   - LinkedIn: "Upload Connections.csv" button. The small (i) icon next to
     it explains how to get the file from LinkedIn.
   - CSV / XLSX: "Upload contacts" for any spreadsheet with names and
     companies.
   - X: "Connect X" to sign in with X; when connected, "Import follows".
   - Attio (firm CRM): shown as "Connect Attio - soon" (coming later).
   - "Remove all" (red, top right of the card) deletes all imported contacts.
   You can also drag a file onto the card.
3. The map controls (one line):
   - Grouping: "By role", "By company", "By industry", "By country".
   - "Find a person or company" box: highlights matches and moves the map
     to them.
   - Right side, small grey text: how many people and companies are shown.
4. The breadcrumb line: "All > Founders & executives > Big Tech > Google".
   It shows where you are on the map and follows you as you move. Click any
   step to fly back out to that level.
5. THE MAP itself: a see-through globe you can spin.
   - Top left of the map: four small buttons: zoom in, zoom out, fit to
     view, full screen.
   - Bottom of the map: two bars, "Zoom to" and "Presets" (see below).
6. Answers to questions appear in a card under the map.

HOW THE MAP IS ORGANISED
- The map is a globe with layers. "You" are the bright core in the middle.
- Groups sit on the inner layer, sub-groups on the middle layer, companies
  on the outer layer and people on the surface. Lines branch outward from
  each one to the next layer, like a burst from the centre.
- The groups are spread all the way round the globe. The globe is
  see-through, so groups on the far side show faintly behind the near side;
  spin the globe to bring them to the front. The largest group starts in
  front.
- By role (the default) the groups are:
     Investors & LPs       (who can fund you or introduce an LP)
     Founders & executives (who runs companies, sees deals first)
     Operators & talent    (who does the work inside companies, plus students)
     Experts & advisors    (who can vet a sector, company or founder)
     Everyone else         (contacts with no title and no company; usually
                           very few)
- Each company is a burst of rays, one ray per person. The most senior
  people (founders, CEOs, partners) are closest to the company's centre.
  Bigger bursts mean more people the user knows there.
- Hollow dots are firm contacts known through a colleague, not the user's
  own connections.

HOW TO MOVE AROUND THE MAP
- Drag to spin the globe. Let go while moving and it keeps turning.
- Scroll (or pinch) to dive in closer or pull back out.
- Click anything (a group, a company, a person) and the globe turns it to
  face you and flies to it.
- Zoomed out, only the main groups are named. Click a group (or zoom in)
  and its sub-groups appear; go closer and the companies appear.
- Labels look different at each level: main groups are solid coloured
  capsules in capital letters, sub-groups are small cards with a coloured
  bar on the left, companies are small underlined plates.
- The closer you are, the more you see: first groups and companies, then
  company names, then each person as a dot with initials, then names and
  titles. Hover anything to see its name.
- Click a company to open a details panel on the right of the map: the
  people you know there, plus "Company profile", "Search company" and
  "Find intro paths to <company>".
- Click a person to see their title, company, role, which lenses they fit
  (Raise, Source, Diligence), their LinkedIn profile link and a "Founder
  check" button.

LAYERS, FROM THE OUTSIDE IN
     By role     -> role, then industry, then companies and people
     By industry -> industry, then role, then companies and people
     By country  -> country, then industry, then companies and people
     By company  -> companies and people

THE DASHBOARD (to the right of the globe)
- It always describes the part of the globe that is in view.
- "Layers" at the top shows the steps, for example Role > Industry >
  Company > People. Click a step already chosen to go back to it.
- A seniority bar shows Leaders, Heads and Team. Click a part of the bar to
  list only those people.
- Three boxes, "Raise", "Source" and "Diligence", show how many people in
  view can help with each. Click one to list only those people.
- "Next layer" shows what is inside as bars. Click a bar to open it on the
  globe.
- The people list shows everyone in view with their title and company. It
  has a "Filter these people" box and can be sorted by Seniority, Recent or
  A-Z. Click a person to fly to them and see their details.
- When you open a group, everything outside it fades on the globe so only
  that group is easy to read.

THE TWO BARS UNDER THE MAP
- "Zoom to": lists what is inside the part of the map you are looking at,
  largest first (for example "Operators & talent 467 | Founders &
  executives 334 | Experts & advisors 246 | Investors & LPs 153"). Click one to fly to
  it. Inside a group it lists the sub-groups, then the companies, then the
  people of one company. The arrow at the start goes back out one level.
- "Presets": "Whole network" (everyone, zoomed out), "Raise capital",
  "Source deals", "Diligence" (each shows only the people who fit that
  purpose), and "Largest company". If firm contacts exist, "My direct
  connections only" also appears.

INDUSTRIES the map recognises include: Big Tech, AI, Fintech, Finance &
banking, Venture capital, Consulting, Software, Healthcare, Education,
Media, Climate & energy, Commerce, Security, Crypto, Biotech, Real estate,
Gaming. People that match none are under "Other".

ROLES the map recognises: LP / allocator, Investor, Accelerator / platform,
Founder, Executive, Operator, Banker / M&A, Lawyer, Accountant / finance,
Consultant / advisor, Researcher / academic, Press / analyst.

=====================================================================
7. FOUNDER CHECK ("/founder")
=====================================================================

PURPOSE: A background brief on one person.

HOW TO USE: type the full name (first and last, exact spelling, e.g.
"Arthur Mensch"), optionally the company to narrow it to the right person,
then click "Run check".

THE BRIEF SHOWS: career timeline, past companies and roles, education,
litigation mentions, press, public profile snippets, a warning when several
people share the name, and "Who can help diligence <name>": people in the
user's network at the founder's current and past companies (for reference
calls and backchannel checks), plus sector experts. Every line is quoted
from, or cited to, a numbered source.

=====================================================================
8. COMPANY PROFILE ("/company/<name>")
=====================================================================

Reached from "Company profile" buttons. Shows funding rounds, investors,
"Your network and <company>", key people, competitors, headcount, other
facts and, for public companies, market data. Every fact is a quoted
sentence with a numbered source. "Search this company" runs Open Research.

=====================================================================
9. WORKSPACES ("/workspaces"), signed in
=====================================================================

- One workspace per deal or company. Create one with a name and company.
- A workspace holds: the searches filed to it, analyst notes, pinned
  sources, a data room, and a watch toggle for the company.
- DATA ROOM: upload deal documents (PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT) and ask questions.
  Answers cite passages as [D1], [D2]... and the passages are shown.
- MEMO: the "Memo" page assembles the workspace's summaries, notes, sources
  and documents into one memo with a single numbered source list.
  Download it as Markdown or Word (DOCX).

=====================================================================
10. WATCHLIST ("/watchlist"), signed in
=====================================================================

- Type a company name and click "Watch company" (or use "Watch" on a
  workspace or company page).
- The site checks watched companies once a day, and on demand with
  "Check now", for new funding, people moves, filings, portfolio listings
  and press from the last 90 days.
- New items appear as alerts with labels (Funding, People, Filing,
  Portfolio, Press), each linked to its source. The account menu shows the
  number of unseen alerts.

=====================================================================
11. HISTORY ("/history"), signed in
=====================================================================

Every search is saved. A saved search can be opened, run again, filed
under a workspace, or deleted.
