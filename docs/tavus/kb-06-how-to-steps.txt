TOPIC: Step-by-step instructions for the most common tasks.
USE THIS WHEN the visitor asks "how do I ..." and needs the clicks in order.

RESEARCH A COMPANY
1. Open "Open Research".
2. Type the company and what you want, for example "Stripe latest funding
   round and investors". Press Enter.
3. Read the Summary, then the numbered sources. Open "Key Insights" for the
   claim check.

SEARCH GOVERNMENT, PATENT OR RESEARCH SOURCES
1. On Open Research, switch on "Gov & regulators", "Patents & IP" or
   "Institutions" in the source row.
2. Search as usual.

SEARCH YOUR OWN DOCUMENTS OR LINKS
1. On Open Research, click "Custom" at the end of the source row.
2. Paste a web address, or upload up to 5 files.
3. Search.

RUN A FOUNDER CHECK
1. Open "Founder Check".
2. Type first and last name. Add the company if you know it.
3. Click "Run check".

IMPORT LINKEDIN CONNECTIONS
1. On LinkedIn: Settings & Privacy > Data privacy > Get a copy of your data.
2. Choose "Connections" and request it. LinkedIn emails a download link,
   usually within minutes.
3. Unzip it and find "Connections.csv".
4. On Research Hub open "Network Map" and click "Upload Connections.csv".
Tip: sign in first so the contacts are saved to your account.

IMPORT A SPREADSHEET OF CONTACTS
1. The file needs a header row with a name column ("Name", or "First Name"
   and "Last Name"), and ideally "Company", "Title", "Email", "LinkedIn URL",
   "Location" and "Tags".
2. On Network Map click "Upload contacts".
A new upload replaces the previous upload of the same type.

FIND WHO CAN HELP YOU RAISE
On Network Map click "Raise capital" in the "Presets" bar, or ask "Who can
help me raise a fund?" in the ask bar. LPs and family offices come first.

FIND DEAL FLOW IN A SECTOR
On Network Map ask "Who sends me deal flow in fintech?" (any sector).

FIND WHO CAN DILIGENCE A COMPANY OR SECTOR
On Network Map click "Diligence" in "Presets", or ask "Who can diligence an
AI company?". A company profile and a Founder Check also show a "Your
network" panel.

FIND AN INTRODUCTION TO A COMPANY
On Network Map ask "Intro paths to Stripe". Results show people who work
there, people at similar names, and people at its investors.

EXPLORE THE GLOBE
1. Click a group on the globe or in the "Zoom to" bar.
2. Click a sub-group, then a company, then a person.
3. Go back with the breadcrumb or the arrow in "Zoom to".

SEE ONLY SENIOR PEOPLE IN A GROUP
Open the group, then click "Leaders" on the seniority bar in the dashboard.

CREATE A WORKSPACE AND A MEMO
1. Sign in. Click "Workspaces". Type the company and click "New workspace".
2. On Open Research set "Save to" to that workspace, then search.
3. In the workspace add notes, pin sources and upload documents.
4. Open "Memo" and download it.

WATCH A COMPANY
Sign in. Click "Watchlist". Type the company name exactly and click "Watch
company".

SWITCH TO NIGHT MODE
Click the moon button at the top right. Click the sun to switch back.
