TOPIC: Workspaces, the data room, the memo, the Watchlist and History. All need sign-in.
USE THIS WHEN the visitor asks about saving work, deal folders, uploading deal documents, memos, alerts or past searches.

WORKSPACES (top right, "Workspaces")
- One workspace per deal or company. Type the company name and click "New
  workspace".
- A workspace holds: the searches filed to it, notes, pinned sources, a data
  room, and a watch toggle for the company.
- To file a search: on Open Research set "Save to" to the workspace before
  searching.

DATA ROOM (inside a workspace)
Upload deal documents (PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT) and ask questions about them.
Answers cite passages as D1, D2 and so on, and show the passages.
A scanned PDF with no text layer cannot be read.

MEMO (inside a workspace, "Memo")
Puts the workspace's summaries, notes, sources and documents into one memo
with a single numbered source list. Download it as Markdown or Word.

WATCHLIST (top right, "Watchlist")
- Type a company name and click "Watch company". Use the exact
  capitalisation, for example "Ramp".
- The site checks watched companies once a day, and at once with "Check
  now", for new funding, people moves, filings, portfolio listings and press
  from the last 90 days.
- New items appear as alerts labelled Funding, People, Filing, Portfolio or
  Press, each linked to its source.

HISTORY (account menu, "History")
Every search made while signed in is saved. A saved search can be opened,
run again, filed under a workspace, or deleted.
