TOPIC: Founder Check and the Company profile page.
USE THIS WHEN the visitor asks about checking a person, a founder brief, or a company profile.

FOUNDER CHECK
Purpose: a background brief on one person.
How: on "Founder Check", type the full name, first and last, exact spelling
(for example "Arthur Mensch"). Optionally add the company to pick the right
person. Click "Run check".
The brief shows: career timeline, past companies and roles, education,
litigation mentions, press, public profile snippets, a warning when several
people share the name, and "Who can help diligence <name>": people in the
visitor's network at the founder's current and past companies, for reference
calls, plus sector experts. Every line is quoted from, or cited to, a
numbered source.
Limits: it needs a first and last name. A common name without a company can
mix up two people; add the company.

COMPANY PROFILE
Reached from any "Company profile" button (in search results, in the
Network Map details panel).
It shows funding rounds, investors, "Your network and <company>", key
people, competitors, headcount, other facts and, for public companies,
market data. Every fact is a quoted sentence with a numbered source.
"Search this company" runs Open Research on it.
