TOPIC: Open Research: the search page, sources, date filter, and how to read the results.
USE THIS WHEN the visitor asks how to search, which sources to pick, or what something in the results means.

PURPOSE
Ask about any company, founder or market and get an answer written only
from cited sources.

THE PAGE, TOP TO BOTTOM
1. Heading "Research any company, founder or market".
2. The search bar and the "Search" button. Type and press Enter.
3. Options under the bar:
   - "Published": Any time, Past month, Past year, Past 3 years.
   - "Save to" (signed in): file the search under a workspace, or "History
     only".
   - "Add new workspace": opens Workspaces.
4. The source row: chips to switch sources on and off. Hover a chip to see
   what it covers.

THE SOURCES
- "All": every source, including Custom. Click again to clear.
- "Curated sources": about 800 hand-picked venture, accelerator, funding
  news, government and research feeds. On by default.
- "Web": general web and news. On by default.
- "LinkedIn", "X", "Reddit", "Substack", "Medium", "Crunchbase",
  "Pitchbook", "Carta": searches limited to those sites.
- "Verified": official filings and market data (SEC EDGAR and government
  sites).
- "Gov & regulators": SEC, Federal Reserve, FDIC, OCC, Treasury, CFPB, FTC,
  DOJ, FINRA, CMS, FDA, HHS, NIH, Census, BLS, state business registries,
  and UK and EU regulators.
- "Patents & IP": USPTO, Google Patents, US Copyright Office, WIPO, EPO; and
  research funders DARPA, NSF, NIST, NASA, NIH, SBIR.
- "Institutions": UN, World Bank, IMF, OECD, WHO; McKinsey, BCG, Bain,
  Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, Gartner; Brookings, RAND, Pew, NBER; SSRN, arXiv.
- "Custom": add your own web addresses or upload files (PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT;
  up to 5 files, 25 MB each) to search with the rest.

WHICH SOURCES FOR WHICH QUESTION
- Most questions: leave "Curated sources" and "Web" on.
- Recent news only: set "Published" to "Past month" or "Past year".
- Filings, enforcement, banking or healthcare regulation: add "Gov &
  regulators".
- Patents, trademarks, research grants: add "Patents & IP".
- Market size, industry reports, think tanks: add "Institutions".
- The visitor's own documents: "Custom".
- Everything at once: "All".

READING THE RESULTS
- Two tabs: "All Results" (the written answer) and "Key Insights" (key
  points with figures highlighted, a quality assessment, the claim check
  and the top sources).
- A badge shows whether an AI wrote the answer or it is "Quoted from
  sources" (direct quotes only).
- The answer has a Summary, Key Points, a Detailed Analysis and follow-up
  questions. Click "See more" to expand it. Small numbers after a sentence
  are its sources; click one to open that source.
- Follow-up questions are chips under the answer. Clicking one runs it.
- Quality assessment: Relevance, Accuracy, Credibility and Overall, as
  percentages for this set of sources.
- Claim check: funding and valuation figures marked corroborated (two or
  more sites agree), single-source, or conflicting.
- Each source has a quality label: Filing / official, Reputable press,
  Blog / newsletter, or Forum / social, and a date.
- "Your network and <company>": who the visitor knows at that company, at
  its investors and competitors, and who in their network covers the sector.
- Buttons open the Company profile or run a Founder check.
- Signed in, every search is saved to History. "Run again" repeats it.
